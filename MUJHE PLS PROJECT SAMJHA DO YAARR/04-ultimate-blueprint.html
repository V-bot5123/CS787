Evaluation checklist (run for baseline + every extension)

1Arbitrage penalty Π — mean/std/median, vs. market data, BCE-only GAN, raw VolGAN.
2Smoothness — visual check against L_m, L_τ.
3PCA structure — inner product of simulated vs. data eigenvectors (paper benchmark ~0.9 for level/skew).
4Correlations & tails — asymmetric heavy tails vs. Gaussian; leverage-effect correlation.
5Quantile exceedance at 1/2.5/97.5/99% (Kupiec test) — the tail-realism check from Step 13.
6Hedging — tracking error vs. Black-Scholes delta and delta-vega.
7Module 1 detection — precision/recall vs. the z-score baseline.
8Survival sensitivity — how the survival ratio moves with loss limit L, re-weighting β, and the latent c.

Every fallback tier, if compute or data forces a cut, drops the Wasserstein-GP ablation and the VAE (use PCA) before dropping anything else — the paper-faithful VolGAN baseline, Module 1, and the survival test are the minimum coherent pipeline.
